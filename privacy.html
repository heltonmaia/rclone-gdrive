Privacy Policy — rclone gdrive

rclone gdrive is a personal-use OAuth client that lets rclone, running locally on the owner's computer, access the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Google user data is accessed only by rclone on the owner's machine, to sync and mount files.
  • No data is sent to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by this project.
  • No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
